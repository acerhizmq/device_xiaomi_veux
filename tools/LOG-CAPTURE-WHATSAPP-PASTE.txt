WhatsApp log capture — telefona kurulum (root shell)
====================================================

/sdcard uzerinde ./script.sh CALISMAZ (noexec).
Script /data/local/tmp/ altina yazilir, sh ile calistirilir.

--- YONTEM A: PC'den push (en kolay) ---

  cd lunaris/device/xiaomi/veux/tools
  bash LOG-CAPTURE-WHATSAPP-PUSH.sh

--- YONTEM B: Root shell'e yapistir (PC yoksa) ---

  su
  cat > /data/local/tmp/wp-cap.sh << 'ENDOFFILE'
  (LOG-CAPTURE-WHATSAPP-PHONE.sh dosyasinin TAMAMINI buraya yapistir)
  ENDOFFILE
  chmod 644 /data/local/tmp/wp-cap.sh

--- TEST ---

  1) WhatsApp goruntulu arama
  2) On -> ARKA kamera gec
  3) Hemen:
       sh /data/local/tmp/wp-cap.sh

  Veya once logger'i baslat, sonra test yap:
       sh /data/local/tmp/wp-cap.sh watch

--- GONDER ---

  Dosya Yoneticisi -> Indirilenler (Download)
  lunaris-whatsapp-logs-YYYYMMDD-HHMMSS klasorunu ZIP yap, gonder.

  Icinde analysis/summary.txt otomatik ozet uretir.
