Video donma / takilma log yakalama — kurulum
=============================================

--- YONTEM A: Windows PC (.bat) — ONERILEN ---

  1) USB debugging acik, telefon bagli
  2) Calistir: LOG-CAPTURE-VIDEO-FREEZE.bat
  3) YouTube Shorts / IG Reels / TikTok'ta donmayi tetikle
  4) PC'de bir tusa bas
  5) Olusan lunaris-video-stutter-logs-*.zip dosyasini gonder

  Toplanan: adb logcat (-b all), kernel dmesg once/sonra, last_kmsg,
            dumpsys (codec, SurfaceFlinger, display, mem, power),
            sysfs FPS/GPU/thermal, getprop, tombstone (varsa)

--- YONTEM B: PC'den telefona script push ---

  cd lunaris/device/xiaomi/veux/tools
  bash LOG-CAPTURE-VIDEO-FREEZE-PUSH.sh

  Telefonda (root shell):
    sh /data/local/tmp/vf-cap.sh          # testten HEMEN sonra
    sh /data/local/tmp/vf-cap.sh watch    # once baslat, 3dk icinde test yap

  Cikti: /sdcard/Download/lunaris-video-stutter-logs-*/

--- YONTEM C: Termux (PC yok) ---

  cp LOG-CAPTURE-VIDEO-FREEZE-TERMUX.sh ~/
  bash ~/LOG-CAPTURE-VIDEO-FREEZE-TERMUX.sh

--- TEST IPUCLARI ---

  - Goruntu donuyor, ses devam ediyorsa: tipik video decoder / SurfaceFlinger
  - Kaydirinca takilma: refresh rate / kgsl / jank
  - Isinma sonrasi: thermal throttle satirlari kernel'de

  analysis/summary.txt otomatik ozet uretir.
